# Banner direction — revised after user feedback

The banner is one contemporary piece of travel artwork that evokes the entire tour. It is not a collection of photographs placed in sections.

- Use a flowing, deliberately illustrated composition with thoughtful transitions through color, light and atmosphere.
- No panels, dividers, inset photos, bottom strips, cutout edges or brochure layouts.
- Avoid the uncanny middle ground of glossy AI photography. The banner may visibly be art.
- Keep a dominant destination and a clear visual hierarchy; secondary itinerary destinations are supporting cues.
- Preserve recognizable landmark forms. A symbolic journey composition must not be presented as a literal geographic viewpoint.
- Keep major subjects well inside the frame to tolerate responsive cropping.
- No overlaid words, titles, labels, logos or watermarks.
- Keep individual daily and highlight images in the program and summary. Do not embed them into the banner or reuse exact files across placements.
- Daily and highlight imagery retains the separate photographic requirement; this artistic revision applies to banners.
- Optional excursions remain optional in the asset manifest and website copy.

The user cited an Iceland/Norway banner as the preferred example. That image has not been provided, so no exact style match is claimed. The Rome revision is a direction sample, not approval of the final style for all 25 tours.
